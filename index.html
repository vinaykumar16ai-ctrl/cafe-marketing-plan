Our 30-Day Marketing Plan

Week 1 – Get Noticed

Post café photos, introduce the team, and promote popular drinks.

Week 2 – Engage Customers

Run polls, customer posts, and special offers to increase engagement.

Week 3 – Build Loyalty

Introduce loyalty rewards and encourage repeat visits again.

Week 4 – Grow the Café

Promote weekend offers and collect customer feedback.

Our goal

Bring more nearby customers into the café and encourage them to return.

Who we’re reaching

Local residents, students, and people who work nearby.

Social media schedule

Monday · Featured item

Share a short video of a drink or pastry being made.

Wednesday · Café moment

Show the café, introduce a team member, or share a customer-approved visit.

Friday · Weekend prompt

Promote a special, event, or seasonal menu item.

Promotions

  • Offer a weekday coffee-and-pastry combo during a quiet time.
  • Give customers a loyalty card.
  • Partner with a nearby shop.
  • Put a QR code at the counter linking to the menu or social page.

Weekly results

Track offer redemptions, loyalty sign-ups, social engagement, and customer visits.
